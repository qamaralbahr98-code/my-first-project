## Main design: "The scholar and the name behind him"
- **Subject:** the best real portrait of the sheikh (P01a), cut out with rembg. Put him on the left third (the text is on the right for RTL). Use a warm, dignified grade and no heavy effects on the face.
- **Text behind him:** the big title, partly behind his shoulder (person matte over the letters).
  - ar: «عالِمُ الحُجرة»
  - ckb: «زانای حوجرە»
- **Name tag:** under the title, a gold tag with dark text.
  - ar: «الملا عثمان عبد العزيز»
  - ckb: «مامۆستا مەلا عوسمان عەبدولعەزیز»
- **Background:** the hujra interior (A02) or the mud house (A01), darkened to 45% and slightly blurred.
- **Small detail:** "1922–1999" in thin gold type near the name tag.
