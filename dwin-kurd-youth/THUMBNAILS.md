Goal: calm, curious and professional. No flags, no angry faces, no weapons, no clickbait arrows.

- Image: a young person seen from behind on a rooftop or hill at golden hour (A01 or A08), looking toward the light, face not visible.
- Giant title BEHIND the person (they overlap the bottom of the letters): ar «الانتماء» · ckb «پێوەبەستبوون» · kmr «AÎDBÛN».

- Large faint "1962" in the background sky (15% opacity) as a hook.
- Logo top-left. Text on the reading side: right for ar/ckb, left for kmr. Person on the opposite side.

## Concept C — Question

## Rules
- At most 3–4 words of big text. Test it at phone size (it must be readable at 160 px wide).
- Fonts: Aljazeera (ar), Speda (ckb), Montserrat ExtraBold (kmr); cream #EFE8DC with a soft shadow.
- 9:16 version: person centred, title in the upper third, tag below it; keep 250 px clear at the bottom.
- Use the same concept in all three languages so the channels look consistent; A/B test A vs C.
